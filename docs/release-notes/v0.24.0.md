# Release v0.24.0

[Home](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.24.0/README.md) · [All releases](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.24.0/docs/release-notes/README.md)

Two Vanilla weapons get their correct poise, saved builds and queries say what changed
when they load, and stopped or out-of-date searches can no longer change your results.
Apart from those two weapons' poise, every number and ranking is the same as in v0.23.0
in both profiles.

## Vanilla

- Black Steel Greathammer's base poise is 7.5 (it was 7) and Hookclaws' is 3 (it was 4).
  Each move's poise is rescaled to match, so Black Steel Greathammer's one-handed R1 is
  13.875 instead of 12.95.
- Idus Knight and Heavy Knight are marked **(Tarnished Pack)** in the class picker, the
  query strip and the command palette.

## Saved builds and queries

- Loading a saved build now fits it to the current game data, the same way a restored
  session does, and a notice names every change: a weapon, affinity or skill the data no
  longer has, a class that was renamed, stats below the class's starting stats, an
  objective that is no longer available, and a profile rule such as a lower upgrade cap.
- The first time you open a profile, the query starts from that profile's default class,
  stats and objective. It keeps how weapons are held and upgraded, the Scadutree
  blessing and how results are listed. Before, the class and stats carried over from the
  other profile.
- Switching tabs no longer stops the check of a loaded build's results.
- **Backup and recovery** says it is checking your saved builds until the check finishes,
  and its Export and Restore buttons say why they wait.

## Searches

- Stopping a search no longer marks unchanged results as out of date, so Compare, Paths
  and Affinity Watch keep their results.
- A late reply from a stopped search can no longer replace the current results.
- Paths and Affinity Watch refuse to start on out-of-date results and say what to do.
- A search started during a CSV export waits for the export and then runs.
- **Use exact allocation** keeps the chosen objective instead of switching to Max AR.
- Selecting another row clears the Compare target, so Paths no longer traces against the
  previous selection's rival.
- The palette's **level N** command starts the trace instead of only opening Paths.

## Corrections to the v0.23.0 notes

Three statements in the v0.23.0 notes were not fully true for that release. They are true
now:

- Error messages no longer repeat rejected text. In v0.23.0 they still repeated up to 256
  bytes of it.
- Each Affinity Watch line has its own dash pattern. In v0.23.0 lines 11 to 13 repeated
  lines 1 to 3; now all 13 differ.
- Disabled controls explain why. In v0.23.0 the row and Build Detail lock buttons,
  **Run updated search** and **Use exact allocation** did not say why during an export.

## Security

- The app checks every field of a request to its native side before it changes anything,
  so a bad value is rejected instead of being silently replaced.
- Dependency updates fix RUSTSEC-2026-0594 (`cookie`) and RUSTSEC-2026-0592 (`url`).

## Data compatibility and limits

The bundled datasets are **`vanilla-1.17.2-r2`** and **`convergence-3.0.3.0-r4`**, with
snapshot schema **6** and runtime model **`aow-routes-effects-v9/exact-v3`**. The Vanilla
dataset changes only the two weapons above. Builds saved on `vanilla-1.17.2` show as made
on an older dataset and offer to load their inputs or recompute.

Convergence remains experimental: none of its values are measured in game. Hold-to-repeat
loops and bow skills stay unmodeled, some conditional status effects are not counted, and
about 1,000 weapon and Ash pairs whose hits differ from the rest of their weapon type use
their type's hits. Ammunition AR is unavailable.

The `.deb`, AppImage and `.rpm` are tested on a virtual display, on Ubuntu 24.04 for the
first two and Fedora for the `.rpm`, and have not been tried on a physical Linux desktop.
Release packages are not code-signed.
