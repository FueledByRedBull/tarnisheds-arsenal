# Release v0.9.1

GitHub release: [Tarnished's Arsenal v0.9.1](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/releases/tag/v0.9.1)

v0.9.1 hardens ranking correctness, removes smoke-test data left by older builds,
and makes broad AR searches and CSV exports substantially faster and clearer.

> **Convergence coverage remains beta; v0.9.1 is a stable application release.**
> Melee weapon AR, status, passives, affinities, compatibility, and extended grades
> remain supported for Convergence 3.0.0.1. Ammunition weapon AR and Convergence
> Ash of War hit/route damage remain disabled until their profile-specific models
> are validated.

- Fix Vanilla Rallying Standard/Commander's Standard searches and large CSV exports
  that could fail on an explicitly unsupported non-damaging chained effect.
- Replace exhaustive Max AR and Max Physical AR stat enumeration with an exact
  relevant-stat dynamic program. Differential tests require identical stats,
  upgrade, score, and five-part AR output from the exhaustive reference path.
- Reuse one AR stat solve across compatible constant buffs and avoid rebuilding
  identical relevant-stat searches for each buff choice.
- Add 25, 100, 500, and 2,000-row CSV controls. Visible rows export immediately,
  and an identical repeated larger export reuses its completed result.
- Add weapon ID/type, requirements, effective numeric scaling, displayed grades,
  and explicit unified/Standard/Somber upgrade semantics to CSV output. Unsupported
  AoW values are blank rather than misleading zeroes.
- Exclude 33 Convergence bows, crossbows, and ballistae from catalog, search, and
  export until ammunition/projectile damage is modeled. Their extracted source
  records remain intact for future implementation.
- Remove legacy `Packaged smoke ...` presets leaked by older release validation.
  Future packaged smoke runs use a unique WebView2/Tauri data directory, delete
  their temporary preset, and remove that directory after shutdown.
- Show horizontal ranking controls only when columns overflow and disable each
  direction when its boundary is reached.
- Extend DTO, Python binding, data validation, export, high-level performance, and
  packaged-release regression coverage for these changes.
