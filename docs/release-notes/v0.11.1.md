# Release v0.11.1

v0.11.1 makes broad Bleed and Ash-of-War searches dramatically faster, corrects
weapon stance/poise presentation, and hardens the new multi-select and comparison
workflows introduced after v0.11.0.

> **Values remain raw model outputs. Enemy defense, negation, resistance growth,
> proc damage, and universal temporary-buff stacking are not modeled.**

- Replace exhaustive stat enumeration for Max AR, Max Physical AR, Bleed then AR,
  AoW First Hit, and AoW Full Sequence with one exact lexicographic dynamic program.
- Compile legal AoW route topology once per loadout and materialize detailed actions,
  hits, effects, and warnings only for retained results. Unsupported future
  non-separable mechanics explicitly fall back to exhaustive search.
- Align route tie-breaks with final ranking and differential-test every objective
  against the exhaustive oracle, including branching routes.
- Reduce a measured 847,960-candidate AoW Sequence work unit from 3,849.750 ms to
  0.700 ms in release mode (about 5,503x) with the same winner and score.
- Regenerate Vanilla weapon metadata from the 1.17 workbook's actual
  `Poise Damage - PvE` values. Build Detail now uses consistent R1/R2 labels and
  reports each selected AoW route hit's poise damage plus the full-route total,
  calculated from `saWeaponDamage × Poise Dmg MV ÷ 100`.
- Remove the incorrect weapon motion-value/AR-derived display and its unused response
  fields. Weapon poise values are independent of a build's attack rating.
- Make weapon type, affinity, and reinforcement filters easier to combine across
  Rankings and Compare; preserve comparison targets through pinning, clearing, and
  workspace navigation.
- Add stat/filter reset controls, starting-class optimization, clearer progress
  semantics, and a 1650×950 default desktop window.
