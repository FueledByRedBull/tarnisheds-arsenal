# Release v0.11.0

v0.11.0 refreshes Vanilla for Elden Ring 1.17, hardens optimizer correctness,
and turns ranked results into a complete comparison and progression-planning
workflow.

> **Convergence 3.0.0.1 coverage remains beta. Enemy-aware damage, resistance
> growth, proc damage, and universal temporary-buff stacking remain explicitly
> outside the calculation model.**

- Refresh the source-hashed Vanilla snapshot for Elden Ring 1.17; add Idus Knight,
  Heavy Knight, and all eight new weapon families: Leontiel's Greatsword, Hefty
  Scimitar, Golden Order Flail, Silver Grooved Shield, Ritual Thrusting Shield,
  Reverse-Bladed Sword, Reed Great Katana, and Idus Sword.
- Recover the 1.17 weapon and native-skill names that are absent from the bundled
  Paramdex metadata while continuing to reject every unknown or NPC-only row.
- Add backend-enforced multi-select checkboxes for weapon types and affinities.
  Selections are combined as OR within each list and AND between the two lists.
- Add Automatic, Per weapon, and Per loadout result grouping.
- Add an eight-build persistent comparison bench with objective, AR, element,
  status, Ash of War, stamina, and plain-language deltas.
- Add truthful no-respec progression paths and an independently optimized
  per-level envelope.
- Expand Affinity Watch with absolute lead, percentage lead, and crossover quality.
- Restore the direct stat-entry model: level is derived from all eight entered
  stats, with no separate target-level or offensive-point mode.
- Open the desktop window at 1650×950 while retaining its existing minimum size.
- Add BuildPreset V2 with strict validation, stable filter IDs, comparison-bench
  persistence, sharing, and migration from existing presets.
- Preserve native-skill and Ash of War route provenance, distinct status curves,
  legal bow-family handing, strict level bounds, fail-closed parsing, and safe CSV
  export behavior.
- Keep the direct measured solver, bounded cancellation, and existing phase timing;
  benchmark results did not justify new caches, schedulers, storage layers, vector
  dynamic programming, or speculative fast paths.
- Move all first-party workflow actions to their supported Node 24-backed releases.

Ash of War motion provenance still uses the available 1.16.1 community workbook.
No 1.17 motion records are invented; replace that source only when an authoritative
1.17 workbook becomes available.
