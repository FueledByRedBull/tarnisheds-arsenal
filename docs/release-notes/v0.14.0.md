# Release v0.14.0

[Home](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/README.md) · [All releases](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/release-notes/README.md) · [Release guide](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/releasing.md)

**Exact rankings. Clear tradeoffs. Faster progression previews.**

v0.14.0 brings exact numeric ranking to the optimizer and strengthens the desktop
when calculations are cancelled or storage is unavailable.
Compare also exposes exact AR / bleed tradeoffs for one selected loadout.

## Exact ranking

- Rank with exact arithmetic, using bounded `i128` dynamic programming and
  arbitrary-precision integers when required. Close results no longer lose their
  preferred stats through intermediate floating-point rounding. Paths and
  Affinity Watch use the same exact numeric ordering.
- Invalidate saved result calculations from the previous numerical contract while
  preserving saved inputs. Displayed numbers remain rounded for readability.
- Use the regenerated `aow-routes-effects-v7/exact-v1` model identity for runtime
  results and caches.
- Regenerate schema-5 snapshots for attack provenance (`is_bullet_attack`,
  `is_throw_attack`, and weapon `critical_damage_percent`); schema-4 snapshots must
  be regenerated rather than relabeled. The extractor identity is
  `phase1-python-v11-attack-provenance`.
- Keep exact reachable-state bounds in the DP; under the same schema-5/model-v7
  `exact-v1` contract, the 200-level case is 16.1% faster. See the
  [performance measurements](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/performance.md#exact-scoring-measurements).
- Avoid repeating stat searches when a loadout has no skill route and its remaining
  metrics cannot break primary ties, improving Convergence bleed searches.

Ranking now treats the loaded coefficients as exact binary values, so winners near former rounded ties
can change. Status floors remain at the same calculation stages, but exact operands
can also change integer buildup near a floor boundary. Convergence remains
experimental with its existing modeling limitations.
See the [numerical contract](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/design/optimizer-math.md#numerical-contract).

## Calculation corrections

- Keep Frostbite, Scarlet Rot, and Death Blight buildup independent of attributes;
  retain reinforcement changes and explicit skill status additions.
- Apply weapon-specific scaling overrides to AR and skill hits, including the
  reduced physical Intelligence/Faith scaling on elemental Smithscript weapons.
- Account for Vanilla Occult Fingerprint Stone Shield losing Madness buildup,
  as documented by the [independent calculator](https://github.com/ThomasJClark/elden-ring-weapon-calculator/blob/b8a1cf8847fe67aacc7f8fcb038a9cfd6725f19a/src/buildData.ts).
- Keep unique-weapon attacks out of transferable Ashes of War. Ordinary Spinning
  Weapon and Spinning Slash no longer include Carian Regal Scepter or Dragon
  Halberd attacks.
- Keep Carian Retaliation's sword bullets independent of stats and upgrades, and
  include Ice Lightning Sword's raw bullet components. See the
  [skill model](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/model-reference.md#skill-damage-and-effects)
  for source provenance and special cases.
- Preserve a native skill's source ID when its localized label or motion rows are
  unavailable. Exact-ID filtering remains selectable with a null label and no
  invented damage rows (including Convergence Stormcaller's Dirk, ID 133).
- Apply each weapon's `critical_damage_percent` only to rows marked by raw throw flag
  `2`. Lifesteal Fist grab rows therefore receive 110% on Katar, Pata, and Raptor
  Talons, while initial contact rows remain unchanged.
- Apply influence offsets and weighted overwrite/scaling terms to the full
  contribution, selecting the strongest negative correction instead of unsafe
  additive aggregation. When no unique primary exists, direct enumeration handles
  the coupled route. The Lifesteal Fist result matches a modern independent
  calculator; an older ERDB result differs. This is calculator agreement only and
  does not prove in-game behavior.
- Preserve exact production status floors, including known boundary differences
  from other calculators. The
  [model reference](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/model-reference.md#known-reference-differences)
  records the Bloodfiend's Fork cases; external status agreement is not universal.
- Keep public AR and bleed display/reference values on the exact production
  evaluators; float AR estimates are used only for scheduling.
- Check every candidate weapon/Ash pair against raw permissions and exercise all
  legal transferable/native configurations at upgrade endpoints in both handling
  modes. Scope and results are recorded [below](#verification).
- Stop reporting an unsupported combined +90 status buff for Chilling Mist and
  Poisonous Mist. Their overlapping weapon/on-hit status effects remain explicitly
  unmodeled because available sources conflict on engine correction and stacking;
  these skills are excluded from skill-damage objectives and carry a warning in AR
  results. These changes do not enable Mist stacking.

## Desktop reliability

- Run loadout comparisons, upgrade series, and preset recalculations on bounded
  native workers. Cancelling the last consumer stops obsolete calculation work.
- Require fresh Rankings results before Compare work. Clear stale comparison
  series/targets, abort remaining solve or upgrade siblings after the first failure,
  and preserve shared cache work for other subscribers.
- Retain search ownership after cancellation or status communication fails.
  Reconcile the existing worker before starting another search, and report when
  its state cannot be established.
- Keep Paths and Affinity Watch on independent queues so uncertain ownership in one
  analysis does not launch or block work in the other. Fixed-loadout Paths clear
  discovery filters before evaluation.
- Load readable saved builds even when comparison pins cannot be persisted.
  Keep comparison changes usable for the current session and show a storage
  warning without modifying the saved build.
- Validate profile capabilities again when a prepared evaluator receives changed
  inputs, including character levels outside the supported range.
- Treat the UI's “Snapshot loaded” label as successful manifest loading and declared
  capability coverage, not verification of every formula.
- Label optimum-envelope transitions as "Respec required" instead of "Added RESPEC".
- Cancel remaining preset recalculations when one migration operation fails.
- Keep ranking Pin and Lock actions visible while scrolling narrow windows.
- Separate the tradeoff scope and locked-stat explanation with readable spacing.

## Tradeoff options

- Compute the complete AR / bleed frontier for the selected weapon, affinity,
  skill, upgrade, budget, floors/locks, and handling settings in Compare.
- Inspect maximum AR, maximum bleed, and the most bleed within 1%, 3%, or 5% AR
  sacrifice. Duplicate choices share their labels. A custom percentage uses exact
  thresholds rather than rounded display values.
- Explore actual attainable points through the linked scatter plot and full table;
  see AR sacrificed, bleed gained, and all stat changes. Apply an option through
  exact locks and the existing build/save actions. These are modeled AR/buildup
  choices, not predictions of bleed procs or enemy damage.
- Explain when locks leave no allocation to vary, and distinguish a single
  non-dominated AR/bleed outcome from a unique feasible allocation.
- Reuse primary formulas, stat contributions, and terminal DP tables across
  compatible fixed-loadout level ranges. Preparation reuse cuts the measured
  200-level case from 42.9 ms to 5.1 ms with identical complete results.

## Maintenance

- Package the portable EXE and documentation in the ZIP; offer the MSI separately.
- Simplify desktop state and native job storage while preserving cancellation,
  stale-result checks, and profile isolation. Paths and Affinity Watch workers
  retain only their selected profile.
- Remove unused frontend API wrappers, duplicate filter/conversion helpers, and
  the redundant path-step score field.
- Validate each Vanilla table once, retaining stricter profile thresholds and
  coverage checks. Reuse the common integer converter during extraction.
- Consolidate desktop styles, test startup, and benchmark tooling. Exclude
  inconclusive samples from reported cancellation latency.
- Upload only the prepared release assets between packaging and publication jobs.
- Refresh the README and guide navigation, with clearer setup and download choices.

### Matched-contract performance

The final comparison against `0374693` used identical corrected snapshots and
exact scoring. Rust 1.97 release builds ran on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D, Windows 11, one
Rayon thread pinned to logical processor 6, with separate target directories.
No builds or tests ran during measurement. Raw samples, hashes, requests, and full
fingerprints were retained locally.

All **16 search fingerprints matched**. Reversed-order blocks used one warmup and
three samples per build; broad-search median differences were about -2.0% to +1.2%,
and the short first-hit case added roughly 0.002 ms. Compatible three-affinity
level ranges used one warmup and five samples:

| Additional levels | Previous median (ms) | Reused median (ms) | Reused min–max (ms) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0.681 | 0.684 | 0.681–0.690 |
| 10 | 2.166 | 1.746 | 1.740–1.770 |
| 50 | 13.605 | 10.486 | 10.435–10.533 |
| 200 | 108.591 | 42.705 | 42.618–43.116 |

Complete results matched across builds and independent/shared evaluation. The
200-level case improved about 61%; this is not a universal no-slowdown or
historical f32-parity claim. Six fixed-loadout frontier cases took 0.57–8.53 ms
including preparation, with identical repeat fingerprints. The Lagrangian-bound
experiment preserved results but was slower and was discarded.

The follow-up against `1a759e2` also reuses invariant primary preparation before
looking up terminal DP results. Two five-repeat blocks per build gave pooled
range medians of 0.702/0.895/1.738/5.126 ms at 0/10/50/200 additional levels, versus
0.711/1.761/10.492/42.859 ms before this change. Complete fingerprints matched;
all 16 ordinary-search fingerprints matched too. Ordinary pooled medians changed
by -1.5% to +1.3%, except the short first-hit case (+0.004 ms). Cache eligibility
and per-level winner selection remain unchanged.

The [performance guide](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.14.0/docs/performance.md#fixed-loadout-reuse-and-frontier-experiments)
records the workloads and boundaries. The
[earlier measurement record](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/0374693639cb66a835be5b4ac0dacb8970c4c2d7/docs/performance.md#exact-scoring-measurements)
preserves the preceding bounded-recurrence improvement and historical context.

### Native probe

The locally built v0.14.0 EXE passed both-profile smoke, Compare, exact tradeoff application,
Paths, Affinity Watch, and saved-build flows. Eight uncached solves took 29.10 ms,
a 26-point upgrade series 4.24 ms, and the four-point frontier 4.45 ms (three-repeat
medians after one warmup, including native communication). Concurrent manifest
commands took 2.11/1.87/1.76 ms respectively and finished first in every sample.
One of three solve-cancellation samples measured 3.62 ms to terminal cancellation;
the other two and all three frontier samples finished before cancellation took
effect. Those samples cannot establish cancellation latency. Separate synchronized
regressions cover cancellation behavior. These local checks preceded the final
presentation and portable-ZIP changes; they did not include an MSI or
certificate-backed signing run. Release packaging separately verifies the final
portable and MSI payloads, startup, and checksums. The published build report
records the source commit, completed package checks, and actual signing status.
