# Release v0.9.0

GitHub release: [Tarnished's Arsenal v0.9.0](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/releases/tag/v0.9.0)

v0.9.0 adds first-class game profiles and ships verified Vanilla and The
Convergence 3.0.0.1 datasets in one standalone application.

> **Convergence coverage is beta; v0.9.0 itself is a stable application release.**
> Weapon AR, status, passives, affinities, and compatibility are validated for the
> supported 3.0.0.1 snapshot. Full mod parity and broader in-game verification are
> still in progress, and Convergence Ash of War hit/route damage remains disabled
> rather than estimated from Vanilla data.

- Add an accessible Vanilla/Convergence switch with profile version and model
  coverage visible beside the workspace.
- Extract 3,189 verified player-available Convergence weapon configurations, 22
  affinities, 125 named Ashes of War, passive status data, and 75,290 exact
  weapon/AoW compatibility rows from the mod regulation and authoritative FMG names.
- Merge base and DLC name tables and safely include mod-added PARAM-only player
  weapons and legal fixed variants, including all four Tricia's Pomander elements,
  while rejecting NPC, dummy, stale infusion, and internal transformation rows.
- Keep every search, background job, cache, result, preset, import, path lane, and
  CSV export bound to one explicit profile so data cannot leak across snapshots.
- Migrate legacy profile-less presets to Vanilla and reject contradictory or
  cross-profile saved builds instead of silently changing their identity.
- Validate each profile as an all-or-nothing checksummed snapshot with source
  provenance for the regulation, name FMGs, and Convergence version file.
- Expose profile capabilities to the optimizer and UI. Convergence weapon AR,
  affinities, passives, status buildup, and AoW compatibility are enabled; its AoW
  hit and route damage objectives remain explicitly disabled until mod-specific
  motion data is mapped.
- Enforce Convergence's single +0–+15 reinforcement path, remove unavailable
  Scadutree controls, and prevent stale presets or backend requests from restoring
  Vanilla-only upgrade/scaling settings.
- Model Convergence row-0 attack-element weapons from their declared attribute
  scaling and nonzero damage components, fixing Galvanic Culling Blade stat
  allocation and AR. At +13 its effective STR/DEX/INT scaling is 1.089/1.386/2.277
  (`B`/`B`/`S++`), and STR 15/DEX 15/INT 35 produces 605.654 lightning AR before
  external buffs. Display the mod's extended `S+` and `S++` grades.
- Preserve Convergence weapon status as fixed values instead of applying Vanilla
  stat curves: Galvanic remains 86 frost, Reduvia 82 bleed, and Scorpion's Stinger
  68 rot regardless of stats or reinforcement.
- Gate extraction and CI with an offline differential reference covering every
  modeled weapon's availability, affinity, requirements, base damage, scaling,
  reinforce type, paired state, and seven status families.
- Extend Rust, Tauri, frontend, contract, E2E, packaged-app smoke, CI, packaging,
  and release-provenance gates to cover both profiles.
