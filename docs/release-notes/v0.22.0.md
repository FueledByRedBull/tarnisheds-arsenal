# Release v0.22.0

[Home](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.22.0/README.md) · [All releases](https://github.com/FueledByRedBull/tarnisheds-arsenal/blob/v0.22.0/docs/release-notes/README.md)

Convergence gains attack poise, the Scadutree Blessing and more accurate skill damage.
Vanilla data and results are unchanged.

## Convergence

- Build Detail now shows attack poise for R1, R2, charged and jumping attacks, read from
  the mod's own animations. Bows, seals and staves show the moves they have; crossbows,
  greatbows and ballistae show none.
- Stance damage on skill hits now includes the weapon's base poise. It was too low before.
- The **Scadutree Blessing** is available, with the same multipliers as Vanilla. It is off
  by default.
- War Cry, Barbaric Roar, Carian Retaliation and Shield Strike now deal damage.
- Hits from bullets spawned on a timer now count, which adds attacks to skills such as
  Phantom Slash and Golden Land.
- A volley that repeats one bullet attack now counts it once, which lowers the totals of
  skills such as Wall of Sparks, Thunderbolt and Flame Skewer.
- Weapons with **No Skill** no longer get skill damage.

## App

- The first Ctrl+K of a launch opens faster.

## Data compatibility and limits

The bundled datasets are **`vanilla-1.17.2`** and **`convergence-3.0.3.0-r3`**, with
snapshot schema **6** and runtime model **`aow-routes-effects-v9/exact-v3`**. Convergence
builds and comparison pins saved with an earlier version are recomputed on the new data
when loaded, keeping their weapon, affinity, skill, upgrade and stats, so their numbers
can change. Vanilla builds carry over unchanged.

Convergence remains experimental: none of its values are measured in game. Hold-to-repeat
loops and bow skills stay unmodeled, some conditional status effects are not counted, and
about 1,000 weapon and Ash pairs whose hits differ from the rest of their weapon type use
their type's hits. Ammunition AR is unavailable.

The `.deb`, AppImage and `.rpm` are tested on a virtual display, on Ubuntu 24.04 for the
first two and Fedora for the `.rpm`, and have not been tried on a physical Linux desktop.
Release packages are not code-signed.
